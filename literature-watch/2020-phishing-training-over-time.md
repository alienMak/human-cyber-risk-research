# When does phishing training stop influencing decisions?

**Source:** [An investigation of phishing awareness and education over time: When and how to best remind users](https://www.usenix.org/conference/soups2020/presentation/reinheimer)  
**Authors:** Benjamin Reinheimer and colleagues  
**Published:** August 2020  
**Source type:** Research paper, SOUPS 2020  
**Added:** October 2026

## The finding

In a field investigation with 409 employees in a German public administration organization, the researchers assessed an awareness program over time and compared four reminder approaches. Improvements in identifying phishing and legitimate emails appeared immediately and at four months, but were no longer statistically detectable at six months. Videos and interactive examples were among the more promising reminder formats.

## Why this matters for human cyber risk

A single post-training measurement can make an intervention look more durable than it is. The important question is not just whether a participant improves, but how much of that change survives after the immediate learning experience fades.

For VISHWAR, this supports studying behavior across sessions rather than interpreting one successful simulation as evidence of persistent resilience. It also raises a design problem: a repeated scenario can refresh a memory of the scenario itself, while a changed scenario may test whether the underlying judgment transfers.

## Where the argument needs care

The paper measures performance on phishing and legitimate-email classification in a particular organization. It cannot establish the ideal reminder schedule for every population or for other kinds of social engineering. A lack of statistically detectable improvement at six months is not proof that every participant forgot the training.

## Question to pursue

What happens if two participant groups receive the same initial simulation, but one receives reminders using familiar scenarios while the other receives changed attack contexts? Which group retains safe decision patterns when the scenario is unfamiliar?
