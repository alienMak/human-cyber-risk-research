# Literature watch

This is a collection of research and reporting that helps sharpen the questions behind VISHWAR: how people judge credibility, how attackers shape decisions, and what we can responsibly measure when people encounter simulated attacks.

The focus isn't on collecting links. Each note should make a specific connection, test a tempting assumption, or identify something worth investigating further.

Papers, preprints, practitioner reports, case studies, interviews and podcasts belong here when the evidence is clear. Findings should be distinguished from interpretations, and a paper's limitations matter as much as its conclusions.

The notes are published as research commentary, not as statements that every interpretation has already been personally endorsed. Claims about VISHWAR's own experiments or results must be grounded in actual records.

[Research notes](./sources.md) are indexed by source to avoid repeats.
