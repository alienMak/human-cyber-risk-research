This entry documents which original publication records were checked before the comparative note [Three results that change how I would design a VISHWAR study](../research-notes/three-studies-on-measurement.md) was written. It is a traceable source check rather than a completed systematic-search iteration.

None of these rows should be tagged “full text appraised” on the strength of a conference page or abstract. The preprint should not be represented as a peer-reviewed journal article without confirmation.

This review should return to the original full texts before adding effect-size tables, precise causal claims or a formal quality rating.
