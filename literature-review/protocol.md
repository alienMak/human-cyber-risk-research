# Literature-review protocol

## Question and scope

How does research published between 1 January 2016 and 10 October 2026 explain security decisions under social-engineering pressure, the effects and persistence of training, and methods for measuring behavior across contexts?

This is a **scoping review in progress**, not a completed PRISMA systematic review.

## Search plan

Search original research in SOUPS/USENIX, IEEE S&P, ACM CHI/CCS, Computers & Security, Journal of Cybersecurity, NIST publications, Google Scholar/OpenAlex/Crossref indexes when accessible, and relevant open preprints. Add relevant incident reports and podcasts in a separate, explicitly non-peer-reviewed evidence lane.

Example keyword combinations: (phishing OR smishing OR vishing OR social engineering) AND (susceptibility OR behavior OR decision-making OR reporting OR awareness OR training OR simulation OR longitudinal OR risk measurement), with separate searches for cyber-risk culture, human factors, scenario difficulty, deepfakes and generative AI.

## Inclusion criteria

- Primary empirical study, substantial review, or method paper relevant to human security decisions
- Publication date within the period
- Original persistent source record and clear study identity
- Enough methods/results information to summarize without speculation

## Exclusion / distinction

Marketing collateral, unattributed anecdotes, duplicated publication records and unverifiable claims are excluded from the scholarly synthesis. Preprints, posters, podcasts and reports may be useful, but must keep their source type. Do not equate clicks, reporting, quiz accuracy, stated intentions and simulated decisions.

## Extraction fields

Publication, year, method, population/sample when available, outcome variables, intervention, comparison, findings, limitations, context, reproducibility, and relevance to VISHWAR. Capture full-text appraisal status separately; an abstract-based entry is *not* a full-paper review.

## Review steps

1. Identify source and canonical URL.
2. Screen title/abstract against inclusion rules.
3. Deduplicate DOI/title.
4. Extract method and clearly attributed claims.
5. Check full text and assess risk of bias before writing definitive synthesis.
6. Record disagreements, boundary conditions and untested assumptions.
7. Update the matrix, synthesis and gap analysis only when justified.

No screening totals or meta-analytic effect sizes are claimed here. Historical review additions must use present-day commit dates.
