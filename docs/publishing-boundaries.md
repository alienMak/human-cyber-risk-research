# What I will and won't publish here

This repository is public. That is useful for sharing research, but it also means I need to be deliberate about what goes into it.

## Reasonable to share

- Research questions and clearly marked hypotheses.
- General methods and experimental designs that are cleared for public discussion.
- Reproducible examples using synthetic or properly approved data.
- Results with enough context to understand limitations.
- Corrections to earlier notes when the evidence changes.

## Not for this repository

- VISHWAR production source code, private architecture, keys, credentials, or customer data.
- Participant records, identifying information, or unpublished sensitive results.
- Confidential PR3CORIA material or third-party intellectual property.
- Potentially patent-sensitive mechanisms before disclosure has been reviewed.
- Claims of completed experiments, validated findings, or external approvals that have not happened.

## How I want to write

I'd rather explain a question plainly than make it sound more important with buzzwords. Notes should separate what I observed from what I suspect, and what I suspect from what I have evidence for.

Commits should describe changes that actually happened. Automation should do maintenance work, not manufacture a contribution history.
