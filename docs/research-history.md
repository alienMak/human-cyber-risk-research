# How this research developed

VISHWAR did not begin with this GitHub repository. The research and planning began in February 2025, and the capstone build began in August 2025. I'm documenting those earlier stages here rather than pretending these notes were committed at the time.

This is a retrospective record. Dates refer to the work, not the GitHub commit date. Where I only know the year or month, I have left it at that.

## 2025-02 — Starting the research question

The initial research and planning began with a question about how people make security decisions, not only what they remember from awareness training.

*Record basis: Project-origin chronology shared by the researcher.*

## 2025-08 — Building the capstone

Development started as a cybersecurity capstone. The aim was to turn social-engineering scenarios into decisions participants could experience, rather than only read about.

*Record basis: Capstone development chronology shared by the researcher.*

## 2025 — Capstone research and first study

The capstone research was completed and an exploratory pilot was conducted with 20 participants. Static training and a standardized phishing exercise preceded VISHWAR simulations. These stages measured different things and should not be presented as a causal comparison.

*Record basis: Capstone and exploratory-pilot discussion; exact month not yet assigned.*

## 2026-04 — Refining behavioral measurement

Work focused on decision patterns, reporting and response timing, repeat-risk events, and the question of what a combined measure can reasonably say about behavior. Some scoring designs were proposals rather than validated findings.

*Record basis: April 2026 platform and dashboard design discussions.*

## 2026-05 — Explaining the research through scenarios

The public-facing framing connected influence tactics, branching simulation decisions, and the Identify, Test, Patch, Verify, Report learning cycle. Educational and school-oriented scenarios became a clearer focus.

*Record basis: May 2026 VISHWAR methodology and school teaser work.*

## 2026-08 — Revisiting what the pilot can tell us

A secondary analysis revisited the pilot and the difference between recognizing risk and acting under pressure. Cross-vector behavior, repeated exposure, and limits of the comparison remained central questions.

*Record basis: August 2026 pilot and manuscript discussions.*

## 2026-10 — Opening a separate public research record

This repository was created to make the shareable research history easier to follow while keeping production implementation and sensitive material out of the public archive.

*Record basis: GitHub repository setup, October 2026.*

## Limits of this archive

These entries are a summary, not a reconstruction of individual workdays. The history may be refined when dated source material is reviewed. Research questions and exploratory observations should not be mistaken for validated product claims.
