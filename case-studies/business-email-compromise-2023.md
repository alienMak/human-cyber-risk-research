# Business email compromise and apparent institutional authority

**Source:** [FBI IC3 public advisory, 24 March 2023](https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2023/psa230324).  
**Evidence type:** Official advisory about a reported pattern, not an individual forensic case.

The FBI describes fraudulent purchasing requests in which actors present themselves as representatives of legitimate businesses. Familiar business identities and ordinary procurement expectations are relevant contextual cues.

The advisory does not disclose how every recipient reasoned, whether each recipient checked the request, or the effect of any training intervention. It should not be used as a psychological experiment or a complete reconstruction of one victim's experience.

**Research question:** Which checks establish that a particular purchasing request is authorized, rather than merely consistent with a familiar company identity?

**Follow-up:** Find a named investigation or court record before adding an incident-by-incident chronology. Compare any verified case with the conceptual distinction between credibility and independent verification.
