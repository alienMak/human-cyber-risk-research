# Contributing

This repository is where I work through questions about human cybersecurity behavior. I welcome corrections, useful references, methodological challenges and suggestions for experiments, especially when they help me reconsider an assumption.

If you find a mistake, please open an issue with the source or reasoning behind the correction. I would rather revise a conclusion than leave an unsupported claim in place.

I want the literature notes, experiments and research reflections to share the approach described in [How I approach research](docs/research-writing-and-evidence-standard.md): begin with a real question, explain what the evidence supports, acknowledge what remains uncertain, and write plainly.

The public work uses appropriately attributed sources and clearly identified constructed data. Private implementation details, unpublished VISHWAR methods and confidential or identifiable data are outside the scope of contributions.

Not every proposed idea needs to become a commit. I care more about a useful and traceable research record than the number of updates.
