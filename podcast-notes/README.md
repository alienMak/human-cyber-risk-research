# Podcast notes: computing, human security and AI

This is a **contextual-source lane**, separate from the peer-reviewed literature review. The subject is historical change: early computing and networked systems, human-focused attacks, usable security, machine learning, generative AI and emerging human–AI decision systems. Older events may be discussed in much newer recordings; record **event date and episode date separately**.

## Evidence rules
- A verified publisher episode page is enough to catalogue an episode, **not** to claim the audio was heard.
- Mark access as SHOW-NOTES-ONLY, TRANSCRIPT-REVIEWED, AUDIO-LISTENED, or UNVERIFIED. Quote or timestamp only after checking the corresponding original transcript/audio.
- Attribute guest claims to the speaker; corroborate historical facts and empirical results with original papers, archival records, standards or primary reporting. An interview is not independent replication.
- Keep observation, interpretation and proposed test separate. Never imply the repository owner listened to or endorsed an episode.
- Avoid unlicensed transcript reproduction. Link instead; paraphrase conservatively.
- Do not publish proprietary research mechanisms, confidential company information, personal data or participant records. Check [publishing boundaries](../docs/publishing-boundaries.md) before committing.
