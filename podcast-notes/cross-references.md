## Rule for upgrading a link

First inspect the actual recording or transcript and identify the speaker's specific claim and timestamp. Then inspect the original research's design and outcome. Only after those checks can a relationship be labelled supported, contradicted or unresolved. Until then, keep TOPICAL LINK or CONTEXTUAL COMPARISON.
